## 🌱 About Scrap2Life

Every year, engineering students build amazing projects — robots, IoT systems, smart devices, automation systems and more.

But what happens after submission?

Sensors, Arduino boards, motors, wires, batteries and other valuable components often end up collecting dust or becoming electronic waste.

Meanwhile, juniors spend money purchasing the exact same components for their own projects.

**Scrap2Life bridges this gap.**

It connects students who have unused project components with students who need them, creating a verified campus ecosystem for buying, selling, exchanging and reusing resources.

## 💡 The Problem We're Solving

- 🔴 Valuable electronic components go unused after project submissions.
- 🔴 Juniors spend unnecessarily on components that seniors already possess.
- 🔴 Finding specific hardware components within a college is difficult.
- 🔴 Completed projects are often discarded instead of being reused.
- 🔴 There is no dedicated, verified platform for campus-level resource sharing.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 🎓 Verified Campus Community
A college-specific ecosystem with student verification through college ID and OCR-based verification.

### 2. 🤖 AI-Powered Smart Listings
Students can upload component images and use AI assistance to generate product descriptions and identify relevant details.

### 3. 🔍 Smart Search
Find components using:
- Component name
- Model number
- Category
- Condition
- Price

### 4. 📢 WANTED Board
Can't find a component?

Post what you need, and connect with students who might have it.

### 5. 🧩 Project Deconstruction
Turn completed projects into reusable resources.

Scrap2Life helps break down old projects into individual components that other students can discover and reuse.

### 6. 🛠️ Build with Senior
An optional peer-to-peer mentorship feature connecting juniors with seniors who have experience working with similar components or projects.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. **Verify:** Students join using their college credentials.
2. **List:** Upload unused components or project hardware.
3. **Discover:** Search available resources or post a requirement.
4. **Connect:** Contact the student offering the component.
5. **Reuse:** Complete the exchange through a campus handover.
